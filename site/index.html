How the PH signal works

We score every posting, we don't filter it

Most US companies never say "open to the Philippines" outright — so instead of hiding roles that are silent on it, every listing gets a score from what its own language suggests:

  • 3 — strong: explicitly mentions the Philippines, international contractors, or hiring "anywhere in the world."
  • 2 — medium: "remote-first," "fully distributed," or contractor-friendly language.
  • 1 — weak: just says "remote" or "flexible hours," with no further detail either way.
  • 0 — none: the posting is silent — worth a pitch, just not a confirmed yes.
The introduction process

What happens after you apply

BridgeDesk reviews every application against the role it was sent for, using the same AI-assisted read the company would eventually see. Strong matches get forwarded to the employer — so employers only pay once someone's actually hired, and candidates always hear back either way.

  • Applications are reviewed within a few business days.
  • Employers are only contacted for candidates worth their time.
  • No fee to candidates, ever — BridgeDesk is paid by the hiring company.
